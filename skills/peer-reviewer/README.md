# Peer Reviewer

A source-grounded workflow for pre-submission review. It selects a small number of relevant perspectives, retrieves their work from the user's Zotero library, builds reviews from the available texts, synthesizes concerns, and supports revision.

## Workflow files

- `SKILL.md`: purpose, principles, review format, and overall workflow
- `phases/phase0-intake.md` through `phase5-revision.md`: intake, retrieval, reviewer profiles, reviews, synthesis, and revision

## Requirement and limits

Zotero MCP must be connected, with relevant full texts available, for source-grounded reviewer profiles. The simulated reviews are structured stress tests; they do not predict an editor's decision. This skill uses theory- or method-based perspectives grounded in the available sources, while the main evidence-grounded writing workflow defaults to general biomedical review perspectives unless source-grounded personas are explicitly requested.
